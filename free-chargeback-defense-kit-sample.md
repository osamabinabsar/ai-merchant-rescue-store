## What this tells you
- Deadlines come from the dispute record, or are computed and labeled ESTIMATED.
- Every evidence row is a real file/ref the merchant supplied; "MISSING" means it
  is not there, not that it was left out of the letter.
- Cover-letter argument paragraphs are generated only from items scored PRESENT.

   `python scripts/evidence_check.py --cases cases/cases.csv --evidence cases/evidence-index.csv --out cases/out/`
   `python scripts/assemble_packet.py --case YOUR_ID --cases cases/cases.csv --evidence cases/evidence-index.csv --out cases/out/`
