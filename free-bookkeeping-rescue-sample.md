## Want this for your own books?
1. Download `bookkeeping-rescue-v1.zip`.
2. Unpack and run against your statements:
   `python scripts/ingest.py your.csv --account "Checking" --out books/ --year 2026`
   `python scripts/categorize.py --ledger books/ledger.csv --rules books/rules.csv --out books/`
   `python scripts/reconcile.py --ledger books/ledger.csv --out books/ --closing "Checking=YYYY-MM-DD:balance"`
   `python scripts/report.py --ledger books/ledger.csv --out books/out/ --year 2026`
3. Resolve any FAILs and review `out/review-queue.csv` before filing.

Not tax, legal, or accounting advice.
