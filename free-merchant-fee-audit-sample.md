### What this tells you

- The parser classified every fee line into a canonical key and a
  NORMAL / NEGOTIABLE / JUNK / ESCALATE / REVIEW bucket.
- "JUNK" fees are small individually (PCI non-compliance, statement fees) but
  are the easiest to remove; "NEGOTIABLE" fees are where the real money is.
- Savings are presented as a range because closing the whole gap is unlikely;
  50% closure is the conservative case.
- The tool also emits an auditable `audit-data.json` alongside the markdown
  report, so numbers can be re-derived, not just read.

## Want this for your own statement?

1. Download `merchant-fee-audit-v1.zip` from the storefront.
2. Unpack and run against your cleaned statement CSV/JSON:
   `python scripts/ingest_statement.py your-statement.csv --out merchant-audit`
   `python scripts/fee_audit.py --statement merchant-audit/statement.csv --out merchant-audit/out`
3. Use the generated pre-filled renegotiation letter at
   `templates/renegotiation-letter.md` with the `out/audit-data.json` figures.

No accounts, no API keys, and no data leaves your machine. The scripts use
the Python standard library only.
